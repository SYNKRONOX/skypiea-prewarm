The per-turn first-call table is in `tables.md`.

**What improved**

- Spreading the airline tools over distinct servers (console mapping) let prediction show:
  turn 2's reservation_lookup was warm on Skypiea in every cell (33 ms vs about 3,000 ms),
  and with lookahead 1 the handover was warm 3/3.
- Lookahead 2 helped the **coding** demo: fewer cold calls (9 vs 12) and less waiting (median
  7.3-8.1 s vs 9.5-10.3 s), at more idle.

**What did not improve, said plainly**

- **Typing hint: no gain.** The first message takes about 39-43 s to "type", longer than
  the 30 s TTL plus the 5 s purge. The hinted pod expired before Enter: first call warm 0/3
  in every typing cell. It only added idle pod-seconds (e.g. airline 201.7 vs 121.9, coding
  332.0 vs 235.0). A fix to test next: refresh the hint at Enter, or give hint decisions a TTL
  that covers typing. Neither is implemented.
- **Enter hint: no gain.** The LLM thinks for well under a second; a start takes 1.6-2.9 s.
- **Lookahead 2 hurt the airline demo**: the handover was cold 0/3 (vs warm 3/3), and the
  waiting median was 8.5 s vs 4.4 s.
- **Skypiea cost more idle pod-seconds in all 12 cells** (4-11x the baseline).
- The first call of a conversation was never warm in any mode, and the coding `shell` call
  was always cold.
- LLM behaviour: in the coding demo 9 calls per cell were written as text (recovered); in the
  airline demo 3 calls per cell failed on a stack (invalid LLM arguments).

**Not done**

- The **live typed conversation per demo**: it needs a human typing, and this run was
  unattended. Command for the morning below.
- **Early connect** (step 6): implemented and tested offline, off by default
  (`platform.gateway.earlyConnect`, `demo/early_connect_ab.py`), but **not measured**: you
  asked me to skip it. The E0 data bound the possible saving at the readiness lag: median
  0.5-0.8 s per tool on kind and on AWS.
