# The Skypiea Air demo world (SYNTHETIC)

**Everything here is synthetic.** Skypiea Air is a fictional airline. Its flights, flight
numbers, timetable, fares, customers, bookings and offers are generated by
`agent/skyair/world.py` from a fixed seed; nothing comes from a real airline, and no airline
data was scraped. The only real facts are the airports: their IATA codes, city names and
approximate coordinates (used to compute distances). Only the generator and its seed are in
the repository; the world is rebuilt identically on every start (`tests/test_skyair.py`
checks it). Customer names are made up from common first and last names; emails use the
reserved `example.com` / `example.org` / `example.net` domains.

```bash
python -m agent.skyair.world        # summary of the world (nothing is written)
```

## Demo-world "today"

The world has its own fixed date, **Monday 11 January 2027** (`world.ANCHOR`), shown in the
console ("demo-world today") and given to Skye in every turn. "Tomorrow", "next Friday" and
fares that depend on how far ahead you book are computed from it, never from the real date.

## Network

- **46 airports**: 38 in India (DEL, BOM, BLR, HYD, MAA, CCU, AMD, PNQ, GOI, COK, TRV, JAI,
  LKO, GAU, PAT, IXC, VNS, IDR, NAG, BBI, SXR, IXB, CJB, VTZ, RPR, BHO, IXR, ATQ, IXZ, IMF, DED,
  UDR, IXE, IXM, plus TRZ Tiruchirappalli, STV Surat, IXJ Jammu, VGA Vijayawada) and 8 abroad
  (DXB, DOH, AUH, SIN, BKK, KUL, CMB, LHR).
- **Hub and spoke**: DEL, BOM, BLR, HYD, MAA and CCU are hubs and are all connected. Every
  other Indian airport is served from its 1-3 nearest hubs (more for bigger cities; the larger
  cities also from DEL and BOM). International routes start at the hubs and a few large
  cities (e.g. COK and TRV to the Gulf). 230 one-way routes, 420 departures a day.
- **Frequency** grows with the size of both cities (DEL-BOM 4 a day; a small airport once a
  day to its hub).
- **Timetable**: 90 days from today plus 21 past days (for flown bookings), all times **IST**.
  Each departure slot has a fixed flight number (`SKY101` ...); a single departure is
  identified by its **flight_id**, the number plus the date (`SKY214-20JAN`). About 0.5% of
  future departures are cancelled by the airline (their bookings are refundable whatever the
  fare); a few departures are full.
- **Block time** = 30 minutes of taxi + great-circle distance at 780 km/h, rounded to 5
  minutes (DEL-BOM 1,137 km → 1 h 55 m; BLR-GOI 483 km → 1 h 05 m; DEL-LHR → 9 h 10 m).

## Fares (INR)

Saver fare per passenger =
- base: 1,400 + 4.1 × km (domestic), 5,500 + 5.2 × km (international),
- × weekday: Friday and Sunday 1.15, Saturday 1.05, other days 1.00,
- × advance purchase (days from demo-world today): 30 or more 0.85; 14-29 1.00; 7-13 1.15;
  under 7 1.35,
- × a fixed factor per departure between 0.95 and 1.10 (from the seed),
- rounded to 10 rupees.

**Flex** = Saver × 1.35 and **Business** = Saver × 3.0 (rounded to 10).

## Policy (what Skye and the tools apply)

| | Saver | Flex | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Change | INR 2,500 per passenger (5,000 international) + fare difference | fare difference only | fare difference only |
| Cancel | no refund | refund minus INR 750 per passenger | full refund |
| Checked baggage included | 15 kg | 25 kg | 2 × 35 kg |

- If Skypiea Air cancelled a flight, any fare is refunded in full.
- Refunds go to the original payment method only (no cash, no other account).
- Extra 15 kg bag: INR 1,800 domestic, INR 3,500 international, per passenger; 1-3 per booking.
- A change keeps the route. There is no First class and no upgrade for points.
- One promo per booking.

## Customers and bookings

- **306 customers** (`C1001`...): about 85% Indian names, 15% international names; Indian
  (`+91 9xxxx xxxxx`) or UK-style (`+44 7xxx xxxxxx`) phone formats; tiers Blue, Silver, Gold.
  A few names are shared on purpose (Skye must then ask for an email or booking code).
- **930 bookings** in mixed states: confirmed (future flights), cancelled (refunded when the
  fare allowed it) and flown (past flights); one-way or return, 1-3 passengers, 0-2 extra
  bags; codes are 6 characters from an alphabet without 0/O/1/I.
- **Persona cards** (fixed, so the console's persona cards, the scenarios and the tests stay

## Places and dates Skye understands

The tools resolve what the customer says: airport codes in any case (`blr`), city names
(`Bengaluru`), old names and aliases (`Bangalore`, `Bombay`, `Madras`, `Calcutta`, `Trivandrum`,
`Vizag`, `Cochin`, ...), forms like `Bengaluru (BLR)` and small typos (`bangalor`). Dates: ISO
(`2027-01-20`), `20 Jan`, `Jan 20`, `20/01`, `today`, `tomorrow`, `day after tomorrow`,
`friday` / `next Friday` (the coming one), `in 3 days`, `Wednesday 13 January`.

## Mapping to the predictor

`CLASS`) is unchanged and the trained `support` profile is used as is (not retrained).
